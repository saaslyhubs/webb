The "Welcome" Blueprint

Don't know what to write? Use this 3-email sequence for your first automation:

Email 1: The Delivery (Instant)

Give them what they signed up for. Say hi, deliver the value, and set expectations for how often you'll email them.

Email 2: The Logic (24h later)

Share your story. Why did you start SaaslyHubs?(example) Why should they care? Build the human connection.

Email 3: The Offer (48h later)

Now that they trust you, show them how you can help them further. This is where you introduce your main product or service.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Buying Email Lists

Never do this. It kills your deliverability and gets your account banned. Build it organically.

❌ Sending Without Testing

Always send a test email to yourself. Check how it looks on a phone!
