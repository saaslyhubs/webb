It's probably not a traffic problem

Here's something I hear a lot: "I'm getting views, but nobody is buying." Sound familiar?

If you've been posting consistently, getting saves, maybe even some followers but making zero sales, the issue is almost certainly not reach. It's that there's no system catching all that attention and turning it into something.

That system is called a Funnel. It's just the path someone walks from discovering you, to trusting you enough to buy. When the path is clean, people follow it naturally. When it's missing or broken, they scroll past and forget you existed five minutes later.

Most beginners build this backward. They create content, hope it leads somewhere, and wonder why the views don't translate. The ones who figure it out earlier build the path first, then drive people down it.

Why the email list matters more than your follower count

I know everyone says this, but it's worth actually thinking about for a second.

Your followers on any platform like TikTok, Instagram, or Pinterest aren't yours. They're the platform's. If your account gets shadowbanned, the algorithm shifts, or the app loses popularity, that audience is gone. You have no way to reach them.

Your email list is the one thing that's actually yours.

A list of 200 people who genuinely wanted to hear from you is worth more than 10,000 passive followers who barely remember following you. When you have something to say or something to sell, you can reach them directly. No algorithm in the way.
