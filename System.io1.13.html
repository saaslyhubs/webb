The 3 critical elements of a minimalist landing page

Strip a converting landing page down and you're left with exactly three components. Not five, not ten. Three.

1. The headline

The headline is not a place to be clever. It's a place to be specific. Your visitor decides whether to keep reading within seconds. If the headline doesn't state the exact problem you solve, you've already lost them. Fluff doesn't build trust, specificity does.

2. The 1-column layout

No sidebars. No navigation menu. No unrelated images. Here's why: every link is an exit. A landing page with a menu bar is a landing page giving people permission to leave before converting.

3. The call to action (CTA)

"Learn More" and "Submit" are conversion killers. They're vague, they carry no value proposition, and they ask the visitor to trust a button that tells them nothing. Replace vague verbs with specific outcomes.
