Phase 1 The Instant Responder

When someone visits your site, they want answers now. If you wait 4 hours, they've already called your competitor.

  • Immediate Gratification: Your pricing or brochure arrives in seconds.
  • Filtered Leads: Spend time only on people who know your services.
  • Professionalism: Look like a big agency, even as a one-person show.

Phase 2 The "Trust Builder" Sequence

People don't buy from strangers. Use a 3-part automation to bridge the gap:

  • Email 1: The delivery of what they asked for + a short "Who we are".
  • Email 2: A success story or a "Before & After" from a previous client.
  • Email 3: The direct Call to Action (Booking link or Phone number).
